Section 1 — What Is It

01

What Is It

A browser-based infinite canvas that draws in a deliberate hand-drawn style.

↓ to dive in
01 — Concept

The core idea

Excalidraw is a free, open-source virtual whiteboard for sketching diagrams, flowcharts, wireframes, and mind maps. It runs entirely in the browser on an infinite canvas — no account, no install.

Category: lightweight sketch tool, not a full design or workshop platform.
01 — Mental Model

Draft, not final design

Every shape renders in a wobbly, hand-drawn style. That's deliberate — it signals "this is a rough idea" rather than "this is the finished artifact."

Think of it as: a whiteboard marker, not a vector design tool.
01 — Key Distinction

Where it sits vs other tools

Figma/Whimsical produce polished, presentation-ready output. Miro is a full workshop platform. Excalidraw trades polish and features for speed and zero friction.

Use it when: the diagram is for thinking, not for shipping to a client.

Section 5 — Limits & Tips

05

Limits & Tips

Where it falls short, and how to get more out of it anyway.

↓ gotchas + tips
05 — Limits

Know before you rely on it

LimitDetail
No cloud save (free)Local-only unless on Excalidraw+
No facilitation toolsNo voting, timers, workshop templates like Miro
Not pixel-preciseWeaker fit for polished UI wireframes
Collab extras paywalledVoice/screenshare/cloud need Excalidraw+
05 — Tips

Get more out of it

Bookmark scene links — the share link captures a specific board state you can return to.
Embed exports — drop exported PNG/SVG straight into Markdown or Notion docs.
Use libraries — the library panel has reusable icon sets so you're not redrawing common shapes.
