Section 3 — Strategy

03

Business & Marketing Strategy

How SpotHopper positions itself and sells to restaurant owners.

↓ to dive in

Business Strategy

  • Niche focus: independent restaurants/bars only, never chains
  • Edge: one bundled contract instead of juggling separate vendors
  • Contract structure creates lock-in (content ownership, multi-year terms)

Marketing Strategy

Core message: "AI does the marketing for you" — low owner time investment (20-30 min/month), platform handles execution.

Sells against DIY social media management and against generic (non-restaurant) marketing tools.
