↓ to dive in
01 — What Is It

The core idea: aggregation, not a new model

Merlin AI, Monica, Sider, and MaxAI.me don't build their own AI models. They wrap existing ones (GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama) behind one login and one subscription, then inject that access into websites you already have open — Gmail, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Google Search.

Mental model: think of it as a universal remote for AI models, clipped onto your browser toolbar.
01 — What Is It

Where Perplexity is different

Perplexity isn't an aggregator wrapper — it's a search engine with AI synthesis and citations built in. It belongs in this comparison because people often choose between "chat assistant everywhere" (Merlin-type tools) and "cited research answers" (Perplexity) for the same underlying need: getting an AI answer fast, in-browser.

Section 4 — Workflows

04

Pick the Right Tool

Three real scenarios and which tool fits.

↓ ver cada caso
04 — Social & Email

Scenario: fast replies on LinkedIn, X, Gmail

Use Merlin AI. Dedicated inline buttons draft replies and summaries directly on those sites — no copy-paste to a separate chat window.

04 — Creative Output

Scenario: need image or short-video generation too

Use Monica. Handles writing, translation, and summarization plus native image/video generation in one place — watch the credit system on cheaper plans.

04 — Research

Scenario: need a cited, verifiable answer

Use Perplexity. Built for research and fact-checking with inline source citations — not a general in-page chat assistant.

04 — Budget / Speed

Scenario: cheapest daily chat, or fastest inline text edits

Sider wins on price for plain daily chat. MaxAI.me wins on speed for highlight-text-and-act edits on any webpage.
