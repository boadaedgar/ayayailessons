# Prompt: Camera Analysis for Content Creation

Copy-paste the prompt below into a new Claude session (run from this `creators` directory).

---

Analyze and recommend cameras for my content creation setup. Research current models and real Amazon US prices (use web search — do not rely on memory pricing).

## My use cases (in priority order)

1. **Parkinson's fitness content** — exercise videos, mostly indoors (house/gym), sometimes outdoors (park, rooftop). Needs: stable footage of a person moving/demonstrating exercises, decent autofocus on a moving subject, usable in mixed indoor lighting.
2. **Training/meeting sessions (ayayai)** — live sessions. If on Zoom, I'd use the laptop camera UNLESS the recommended camera works as a plug-and-play USB webcam — then it replaces the laptop camera. **USB webcam mode (UVC, no capture card) is a HARD REQUIREMENT for at least one of the two cameras.**
3. **Social content** — Instagram (Reels, vertical 9:16) and YouTube (horizontal). Both video and photos.

## Budget — two tiers

- **Tier 1 (daily driver): under $150** — for everyday shooting.
- **Tier 2 (quality cam): up to $600** — for when better results are required.
- Each budget must cover **camera + SD card + spare battery**. Tripod, mic, and lights are already owned or budgeted separately.

## What I already own

- Moto G8 phone (2020 budget phone — weak camera)
- Tripod
- HMKCH wireless lavalier mic (phone/computer, USB-C/lightning/3.5mm receiver) — https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09TD7WHKZ
- Laptop with built-in webcam

## Purchase context

- Buying from **Amazon US** (new). Real current prices, links if possible.
- Warranty and reliability matter — I can't easily return/service internationally.

## What I want from the analysis

1. **Top 3 candidates per tier** with a comparison table: price (camera + card + battery total), video specs (max res/fps, stabilization, autofocus), photo quality, USB webcam support (native UVC yes/no), mic input (does my lavalier plug in?), battery life while recording, vertical video support, overheating/recording limits.
2. **One clear pick per tier** with reasoning tied to MY use cases (fitness demos indoors, occasional outdoor, webcam for Zoom, Reels/YouTube).
3. **Honest check:** for Tier 1, tell me if a $150 camera actually beats upgrading my phone (e.g., used phone or accessories) — don't recommend a camera just because I asked for one.
4. **Compatibility check:** confirm my HMKCH lavalier works with each pick (3.5mm input? USB?), and what adapter is needed if not.
5. **Gotchas list:** recording time limits, overheating for long fitness sessions, extra costs I'm not seeing (adapters, cages, ND filters).
6. **Total cost of ownership per tier** — everything I'd actually pay to shoot on day one.

Deliver as a comparison document I can review. Ask me before buying decisions are final — this is analysis, not purchase.
